How it works

From assessment to personalised program
in three guided steps

No guesswork. No random activities. NumeriFlow guides your child through a clear, intelligent journey that adapts to how they actually learn.

🎯
Step 1

Game-based assessment

Your child plays a short sequence of interactive games. In the background, we observe accuracy, response time, hesitation, and rhythm — building a picture of their numerical strengths.

📊
Step 2

Learning profile

After the assessment, you receive a clear, friendly learning profile. No harsh scores — just a plain-language summary of what your child finds easy and where they'd benefit from support.

🗺️
Step 3

Personalised program

NumeriFlow builds a tailored learning plan — with activities, music therapy sessions, and level assignments matched exactly to your child's profile. It evolves as they progress.

Understanding dyscalculia

The learning challenge most people haven't heard of

Dyscalculia is a specific learning difficulty that affects a child's ability to understand and work with numbers. It's not about intelligence — many children with dyscalculia excel in other areas.

Like dyslexia with reading, dyscalculia affects how the brain processes numerical information. Early identification and the right support can make an enormous difference.

Difficulty counting consistently or recognising quantities
Struggle to compare numbers or understand which is bigger
Slow response to simple arithmetic despite effort
Difficulty telling the time or understanding patterns
Anxiety around numbers or maths-related tasks
