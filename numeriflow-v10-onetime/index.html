Understanding dyscalculia

The learning challenge most people haven't heard of

Dyscalculia is a specific learning difficulty that affects a child's ability to understand and work with numbers. It's not about intelligence — many children with dyscalculia excel in other areas.

Like dyslexia with reading, dyscalculia affects how the brain processes numerical information. Early identification and the right support can make an enormous difference.

Difficulty counting consistently or recognising quantities
Struggle to compare numbers or understand which is bigger
Slow response to simple arithmetic despite effort
Difficulty telling the time or understanding patterns
Anxiety around numbers or maths-related tasks

Meet the Founder

Saira Mohsin

Founder · Numeriflow Kids Learning Ltd

Saira Mohsin is the founder of NumeriFlow and a specialist Teacher's Assistant with hands-on experience supporting children with varied learning needs — including dyscalculia, dyslexia, and other numeracy challenges — at a Dubai-based British primary school.

Her work with The Citizens Foundation since 2012, supporting children with learning disabilities in Dubai and Karachi, showed her firsthand how early, accessible support changes outcomes. She holds an MBA from the Institute of Business Administration, Karachi, and founded NumeriFlow to make game-based assessment and music therapy available to every family — not just those who can afford a specialist.

"Every child I've worked with had the potential to thrive in maths. They just needed the right support at the right time — and that's exactly what NumeriFlow is built to provide."

— Saira Mohsin, Founder
